Genshin Impact

Genshin Impact takes place in the world of Teyvat, composed of seven major nations, each ruled by a god called an "Archon" and tied to a different element and ideal.

Historia

The protagonists are the Traveler and Paimon, the Traveler's guide and companion. The Traveler and their twin sibling traveled across planets before being separated in Teyvat, forcing the Traveler to go on a journey to find them. The primary antagonists are Celestia, Teyvat's ruler, the Abyss Order, a legion of monsters made up of corrupted Khaenri'ahns who seek to overthrow the existing world order,and the Fatui, a delegation of diplomats from Snezhnaya who pursue an aggressive foreign policy throughout Teyvat. The Traveler's twin is the leader of the Abyss Order, while the Fatui are led by the Eleven Harbingers, powerful lieutenants of the Tsaritsa Anastasya (the Cryo Archon) who have been given special authority to carry out her will.

Sinopsis

Prologo: El forastero que atrapó el viento

El Viajero llega a Mondstadt tras ser rescatado de una emergencia y conoce a Venti y a Dvalin (Stormterror), el dragón corrompido por lo que el grupo investiga la corrupción de Dvalin y coopera con los Caballeros de Favonius,purificando la lira sagrada, se enfrentan a Dvalin en su guarida y se libera al dragón de la influencia del Abismo

Capítulo I: Adiós, amo perfeccionado

El Viajero llega a Liyue con el objetivo de asistir al Rito de la Descensión, una ceremonia anual donde el Arconte Geo (Rex Lapis) baja a dar instrucciones comerciales y de gobierno a los mortales. Sin embargo, durante el rito, el cuerpo de Rex Lapis cae del cielo "muerto", lo que desata el caos, la sospecha hacia los extranjeros y una investigación por parte de las Siete Estrellas de Liyue

Capítulo II: Omnipresencia sobre los mortales

La nación está gobernada por la Raiden Shogun (Ei), quien ha impuesto el Decreto Sakoku (cerrando las fronteras) y el Decreto de Captura de Visiones, confiscando los poderes elementales de los ciudadanos para colocarlos en una estatua gigante (el Dios de los Mil Ojos).

Capítulo III: La verdad entre las páginas del Purana

La Academia de Sumeru ignora a su Arconte Dendro (Nahida) porque veneran y recuerdan a su fallecida predecesora, la Gran Señor Rukkhadevata. Para ello, la tienen cautiva en el Sagrario de Surasthana y usan el sistema del Akasha (un dispositivo tecnológico que recopila y distribuye conocimiento) para controlar los pensamientos y sueños de la población.

Personajes

Archons

Venti - Anemo Archon

Venti, also known by his divine name Barbatos, is a wandering bard who is secretly the God of Freedom and Anemo Archon of The Seven.Venti was originally a single tiny thread among the thousand winds who participated in the rebellion against Decarabian. After the death of all remaining gods in Mondstadt, he was chosen to ascend as the Anemo Archon. Fearing that he may become a tyrant similar to Decarabian, he quickly stepped down from public rule soon after laying the foundations for the nation. He has thereafter spent most of his time either asleep or wandering the world as a bard, only appearing to intervene as an Archon when Mondstadt is in a time of need. He appeared in this way during Vennessa's rebellion against the corrupt aristocracy, as well as during the Cataclysm.

Zhongli - Geo Archon

Zhongli, also known by his divine name Morax and his Adeptus name Rex Lapis, is a Wangsheng Funeral Parlor consultant who serves as the God of Contracts and Geo Archon of The Seven, presiding over Liyue. After faking his death during the last Rite of Descension, he has now stepped down from public rule and left the governance of Liyue in the hands of humanity. Along with Venti, he is one of The Seven's two remaining original members and the oldest of The Seven. He is the Prime of Adepti and was born during the era of the unified civilization. During the Archon War, he founded the nation of Liyue, and eventually defeated all other gods in the region, earning him the title of Geo Archon.

Raiden Ei - Electro Archon

Raiden Ei, also known by her divine name Beelzebul and as the Raiden Shogun, is the God of Eternity and Electro Archon of The Seven, presiding over Inazuma. Ei's consciousness currently resides within a puppet body that has its own independent personality, known as the Shogun. Ei and Makoto were twin gods who won the Archon War together around 2,000 years ago. When Makoto ascended to become Electro Archon, Ei became her kagemusha, or body double. As far as the world was aware, there was only one god named Baal, the Raiden Shogun of Inazuma. After Makoto's death during the cataclysm 500 years ago, Ei continued to rule under the name Baal. She kept her consciousness inside the Plane of Euthymia, leaving the Shogun puppet to control the daily affairs of Inazuma.

Furina/Focalors - Hydro Archon

Furina de Fontaine is a Fontainian celebrity who impersonated the identity of Focalors, the Hydro Archon, on the latter's behalf as part of a plan to save their people from the Prophecy of Fontaine. She is the human body and spirit of Focalors that the latter left behind after seperating her divinity and placing it into the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale. Focalors instructed Furina to fool the world into believing that she was the Hydro Archon, promising her that Fontaine would be saved from the prophecy if she could successfully do so. Furina dutifully performed this role for 499 years, until she was eventually exposed and put to trial before the Opera Epiclese. After Neuvillette chose to absolve the Fontainians of their sin with his restored Authority, thus saving them from being dissolved in the flood, Furina legally abdicated from the duties of Hydro Archon, and moved out from the Palais Mermonia. She now continues to live autonomously as an ordinary human.
